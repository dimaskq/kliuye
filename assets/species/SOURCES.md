# Species pictures

Every picture is in the public domain. All but one are hand-coloured engravings
from Marcus Elieser Bloch, _Ichthyologie, ou Histoire naturelle des poissons_
(Berlin, 1782–1795) and _Systema ichthyologiae_ (1801), as scanned and published
on Wikimedia Commons. The bluefish, which Bloch never drew, is a NOAA drawing.

Each was cut out of its plate, freed of the paper, scaled to 320 px wide and
saved as an 8-bit transparent PNG. Fish face right; the turbot keeps its eyes on
its own (left) side, as the species does.
