Kliuye (Клює) — Privacy Policy

Effective 22 September 2026 · Applies to app version 1.0 and later

English Українська Български

English

Kliuye (“the app”) is a fishing bite forecast for Android and iOS, made by an individual developer, Dmytro Kravchenko (“I”, “me”). This policy explains what the app does with information about you. Short version:

  • There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.
  • I run no servers. I never receive your journal, photos, name or saved places — they stay on your phone.
  • To show a forecast, the app sends approximate coordinates to a weather service. Maps and place names come from map providers, which see the area you look at.

1. Information that stays on your device

The following is stored only in the app’s private storage on your phone and is never sent to me or anyone else by the app:

  • your fishing journal: dates, species, weights, places, notes, and the photos and videos you attach;
  • your profile name and profile photo;
  • saved and recent places, and the names you give them;
  • settings, the last downloaded forecast and cached map tiles for offline use;
  • bite alerts, which are scheduled on the device as local notifications (no push service or push token is used). While alerts are on, the app keeps the chosen place, its name, the species and the language on the device, and about every 9 hours refreshes the forecast for that place in the background — the same rounded coordinates sent to Open-Meteo as when the app is open. Turning alerts off deletes this and stops the refresh.

Photos and videos are chosen through the system photo picker, so the app has no access to the rest of your library and does not request camera or microphone access.

2. Location

If you allow it, the app reads your approximate location (Android “approximate location”, iOS “while using the app”) to show the forecast for where you are. The app works fully without this permission — you can pick a place on the map or search for a town instead. Location is never read in the background, and the device position is not stored.

3. Information sent to third parties

To work, the app talks directly to the services below. Each receives your device’s IP address as part of any internet request, plus the data listed. None of it is linked to your identity by the app, and nothing is sold or used for advertising.

Service What it receives Why
Open-Meteo (weather, marine and place-search APIs) Coordinates of the place you look at, rounded to 0.01° (about 1 km); the text you type into the town search; the app language Weather forecast, water temperature, finding towns
Your phone’s built-in geocoder — Google on Android, Apple on iOS (Google policy, Apple policy) Coordinates of a pin you drop on the map, or of your approximate location Naming the nearest town, so you see a name instead of coordinates
Map tiles — OpenFreeMap, and OpenStreetMap for the offline map The map area being displayed or saved for offline use Drawing the map
Your maps app (Google Maps or Apple Maps) The destination, only when you tap “Route” Directions — the route is built by that app, not by Kliuye

These providers process the data under their own privacy policies, linked above.

4. What the app does not do

  • No accounts or sign-in, so nothing is collected to create one.
  • No analytics, crash reporting, advertising SDKs or advertising identifiers.
  • No tracking across apps or websites, and no sale or sharing of personal data.

5. Backups

Android may include the app’s local data in your personal device backup to your Google account, and iOS in your iCloud or computer backup. These backups are controlled by you and by Google or Apple; I have no access to them.

6. Keeping and deleting your data

Because your data lives only on your device, you are in control of it: delete journal entries or saved places in the app, remove your profile photo in “My profile”, clear the offline map in Settings, or uninstall the app to remove everything. There is no copy on any server of mine to delete.

7. Your rights

If you are in the European Union, the UK or another place with similar laws, you have rights to access, correct, delete and object to processing of your personal data. Since I do not hold any of your personal data, most of these rights are exercised directly on your device as described above. For data processed by the third-party services in section 3, contact them via their policies. You can always write to me with questions, and you may lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

8. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.

9. Changes

If the app starts doing something new with data — for example, showing ads or offering in-app purchases — this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the effective date above will change.

  • ім’я та фото профілю;
  • збережені й недавні місця та назви, які ви їм дали;

Фото й відео вибираються через системний пікер, тож застосунок не має доступу до решти вашої галереї й не просить доступу до камери чи мікрофона.

Якщо ви дозволите, застосунок зчитує приблизне місцеположення (на Android — «приблизне», на iOS — «під час використання»), щоб показати прогноз там, де ви є. Для роботи застосунок напряму звертається до сервісів нижче. Кожен отримує IP-адресу вашого пристрою (як і будь-який інтернет-запит) та перелічені дані. Застосунок не пов’язує їх з вашою особою, нічого не продає й не використовує для реклами.
